Piano Practice Tools

Free, open source, and they run in your browser.

  • Ear TrainerLearn to recognize notes, intervals and Für Elise melodies by ear.
  • Note ReaderLearn to read treble and bass clef notes quickly, and find them on the piano.
  • Fingering CoachWhich finger goes on which key: hand positions, thumb-under, position shifts, and Für Elise fingering.
  • Play by EarHear the opening of 290 famous tunes from around the world, then find the notes on the keyboard.
