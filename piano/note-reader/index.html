Memory aids

  • Treble clef lines (bottom to top) are E G B D F: Every Good Boy Does Fine. The spaces spell FACE.
  • Bass clef lines are G B D F A: Good Boys Do Fine Always. The spaces are A C E G: All Cows Eat Grass.
  • Middle C sits on one ledger line, below the treble staff or above the bass staff.
  • Aim for speed as well as accuracy: answering correctly in under 2 seconds means you can read it while playing.
  • Use Find the key to learn where each note is on the piano, including which octave.

Keys: press C D E F G A B to name a note, and hold Shift for a sharp (Shift+D = D♯).
