How it works

  • Press Start to hear the opening of a famous tune. The first note is given. Find the rest on the keyboard, one note at a time.
  • Hum it first. If you can sing it, you can find it: is the next note higher or lower, a small step or a jump?
  • Stuck? Next few notes plays just the part you're on. Hint reveals one note and counts as a mistake. After 3 wrong tries a note is revealed for you.
  • A song is perfect when you play it with no mistakes and no hints. Songs you haven't got perfect come up more often.
  • Turn off Show the title first to guess the tune too. Turn on Random key once the original keys feel easy.

Keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; play the keyboard from its first C upward. Hold Shift for an octave higher. All tunes are public domain (traditional, or published before 1929). Each one is a short opening phrase, sometimes simplified.
