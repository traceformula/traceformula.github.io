How to practise

  • Do 10 minutes a day. Short daily practice works better than one long session a week.
  • Before answering, sing or hum the note. If you can sing it, you'll recognize it.
  • When you get one wrong, you'll hear your choice. Compare it with the right answer.
  • When you score 90% on the last 20 questions, move up a level.
  • The Für Elise levels in Notes and Melodies train the notes and phrases of the piece you're learning.
  • In Melodies, the first note is given. Sing the melody back in your head, then play it note by note.

Keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J = C to B, K O L P ; = C to E an octave higher (Melodies), 1–9 pick an interval. No sound on iPhone? Turn up the media volume. It should work in silent mode too.
