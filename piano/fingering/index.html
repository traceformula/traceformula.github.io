Hand basics

  1. Finger numbers are the same on both hands: 1 = thumb, 2 = index, 3 = middle, 4 = ring, 5 = pinky.
  2. Sit so your forearms are level with the keys and your elbows hang loosely just in front of your body. Sit on the front half of the bench.
  3. Curve your fingers as if you're holding a small ball or an orange. Play on the fingertips. Fingers 1 and 5 touch with their sides.
  4. Keep the wrist level and loose: not drooping below the keys, not raised high. Any tension means slow down.
  5. One finger per key in a five-finger position. Fingers that aren't playing rest lightly on their keys.
  6. Thumb under / finger over is how you travel beyond five notes without lifting the hand. Try the C scale exercises.

No piano yet? Rest your hand on a table in the curved shape. Use Watch at 40–60 BPM and tap the glowing finger. Lifting one finger at a time while the others stay down trains finger independence. Keys: 1–5 pick a finger.

A teacher can check your posture in a few lessons, which an app can't see. Bad habits are much easier to prevent than to fix.
